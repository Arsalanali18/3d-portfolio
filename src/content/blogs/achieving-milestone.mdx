# Finally Adding a Blog to My Portfolio

After a long battle between motivation, self-doubt, and unfinished side projects, I finally did it, I added a blog section to my portfolio. It may not look revolutionary, but considering how long it lived in my “I’ll do it later” list, this feels like a win.

## The Journey

The idea was simple: “A blog would make my portfolio feel more complete.”  
What followed was weeks of thinking about layout, fonts, and whether anyone would ever read it, followed by a short burst of actual coding that somehow made everything work.

As usual.

### Why This Design?

Because I like clean interfaces, dark themes, and things that don’t scream for attention. The goal was to keep it minimal, readable, and focused on the content, not animations fighting for your CPU.

## What Will I Write About?

- **Things I’m Learning**: Frontend, React, Next.js, and all the small lessons that don’t show up in tutorials.
- **Project Breakdowns**: What worked, what didn’t, and what I’d definitely do differently next time.
- **Developer Thoughts**: Honest takes from someone still figuring things out.

## Why Even Start a Blog?

Because writing forces clarity. If I can explain something simply, it usually means I understand it. And if I can’t, that’s a sign I need to learn more.

## Conclusion

This blog is less about teaching and more about documenting progress. If it helps someone else along the way, that’s a bonus. For now, I’m just glad it exists, and renders without breaking the build.
